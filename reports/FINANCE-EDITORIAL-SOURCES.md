# Future football finance topics

Source review conducted on 14 November 2026. The Spanish topic catalog lives in [finance-topics.json](../actions/5-editorial/finance-topics.json). The evidence below supplies the initial reporting questions. Check the applicable season, editions, and publication dates when each story is prepared.

| Topic | Evidence and scope |
| --- | --- |
| UEFA squad cost | Article 94 sets the 70% threshold. Use Article 93 to explain the ratio and the season-end decisions to illustrate enforcement. [2026 regulation](https://documents.uefa.com/r/UEFA-Club-Licensing-and-Financial-Sustainability-Regulations-2026/Article-94-Squad-cost-rule-Online?contentId=ECUHs41EOPHcoQeGRa3OSA) · [Article 94 HTML](https://documents.uefa.com/api/khub/maps/N_SrSLk1~YFaXcjvn3uSCw/topics/ECUHs41EOPHcoQeGRa3OSA/content) · [2025/26 monitoring decisions](https://www.uefa.com/news-media/news/02a6-20fe598acc34-5b167fbb1b19-1000--finalisation-of-club-monitoring-for-the-2025-26-season/) |
| Premier League financial rules | The league dates the introduction of Squad Cost Ratio and Sustainability and Systemic Resilience to 2026/27. Explain the 85% threshold alongside the additional headroom and its conditions. [League explainer](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4467022/new-premier-league-financial-system-explained) · [Clubs' vote](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4468470/premier-league-statement-new-financial-system) |
| LaLiga squad limits | The transparency register explains club-specific approved limits and links current and archived releases. The financial report gives actual aggregate squad costs for a completed season. Label the two measures and dates when comparing clubs. [Squad limits](https://www.laliga.com/transparencia/gestion-economica/limite-coste-plantilla) · [2024/25 financial report](https://assets.laliga.com/assets/2026/03/18/originals/5658090da03d3a5134540accdc341c89.pdf) |
| LaLiga television rights | Article 5 of the consolidated decree gives the division split and club-level criteria. Use the league's published figures for the same season and division in a comparison. [Consolidated decree](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2015-4780) · [League distribution tables](https://www.laliga.com/transparencia/gestion-economica/derechos-audiovisuales) |
| Champions League payments | The circular sets out the 2025/26 participation, performance and value components. The UEFA distribution page links season-specific documents. Treat estimated distributions as estimates. [2025/26 circular](https://editorial.uefa.com/resources/029a-1e0b5460b86d-31e6cad26358-1000/20250616_circular_2025_32_en.pdf) · [UEFA distribution index](https://www.uefa.com/running-competitions/financial-distribution/mens-competitions/) |
| UEFA payments to non-competing clubs | UEFA's season-specific explainer shows the association allocation, including a competitive balance component. Its standing overview records which clubs qualify and the historical funding series. [2025/26 explainer](https://www.uefa.com/news-media/news/02a7-212bf41887e6-459c11329667-1000--solidarity-payments-to-non-competing-clubs-strengthening-com/) · [Solidarity overview](https://www.uefa.com/development/our-support/solidarity-payments/) |
| Men's World Cup club benefits | FIFA announced a US$355m fund for the 2026 edition, covering qualifying matches and the final tournament. The 2022 release records the amount that was distributed and the number of beneficiary clubs. [2026 distribution model](https://inside.fifa.com/organisation/media-releases/landmark-club-benefits-programme-world-cup-2026) · [2022 payments](https://inside.fifa.com/legal/media-releases/440-clubs-globally-receive-share-of-over-usd-200m-in-fifa-world-cup-2022-tm) |
| Women's World Cup prize money | FIFA's 2023 payout schedule identifies the sums earmarked for associations and individual players. The impact study shows where preparation and club-release funding fit in the package. Use separately dated evidence for any 2027 announcement. [2023 payment model](https://inside.fifa.com/tournaments/womens/womensworldcup/australia-new-zealand2023/media-releases/new-payment-model-guarantees-support-for-every-fifa-womens-world-cup-2023-tm) · [2023 impact study](https://inside.fifa.com/womens-football/fwwc-impact/contributing-areas) |
| Women's international transfer spending | FIFA published US$12.3m in its 2025 midyear snapshot and US$28.6m in its 2026 midyear snapshot. Both refer to international transfers in the professional women's game. Keep each window's dates explicit. [2025 snapshot](https://inside.fifa.com/transfer-system/media-releases/global-transfer-market-new-all-time-highs-2025-mid-year-window) · [2026 snapshot](https://inside.fifa.com/transfer-system/media-releases/world-cup-2026-impact-mid-year-transfer-window) |
| Club agent fees | FIFA reports US$709.6m for 2024 and US$1.37bn for 2025 in fees for club agents in men's professional international transfers. Check the observation periods and use the reports' scope when describing the change. [2024 agents report summary](https://inside.fifa.com/transfer-system/agents/news/fifa-publishes-2024-annual-report-on-football-agents) · [2025 agents report summary](https://inside.fifa.com/transfer-system/agents/news/club-agent-service-fees-reach-new-heights-2025) |
| Transfer fee amortisation | UEFA Annex G.3 supplies the regulatory treatment of the capitalised cost of a permanent registration. Barcelona's intangible-assets note is a club example. Identify the accounting expense and any illustrative cash payment schedule separately. [UEFA Annex G.3](https://documents.uefa.com/r/UEFA-Club-Licensing-and-Financial-Sustainability-Regulations-2026/G.3-Accounting-requirements-for-the-permanent-transfer-of-a-player-s-registration-Online?trk=article-ssr-frontend-pulse_little-text-block) · [Annex G.3 HTML](https://documents.uefa.com/api/khub/maps/N_SrSLk1~YFaXcjvn3uSCw/topics/mbp0thahzVgyytKVZPxyiQ/content) · [Barcelona accounts](https://www.fcbarcelona.com/fcbarcelona/document/2026/10/26/d2cb5a4c-6369-4fbb-87e9-7f500c23a5b3/Indicador-47_CAST.pdf) |
| Camp Nou financing | Barcelona's initial disclosure covers the funding structure and the role of projected stadium income. The later €300m issue specifies a purpose and terms of its own. Check the relationship between facilities before summing announced amounts. [Initial financing](https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/news/3241290/most-important-finance-project-in-fc-barcelona-history-set-in-motion) · [€300m issue](https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/club/news/4608721/fc-barcelona-finalises-300-million-euro-issue-for-completing-spotify-camp-nou-works) |
| LaLiga Impulso and CVC | The league earmarks at least 70% for growth investment. Its transparency page defines allowable uses and links progress reports. The assembly release records the original approval and plan. [Transparency disclosures](https://www.laliga.com/laliga-group/transparencia-laliga-impulso) · [Assembly announcement](https://www.laliga.com/noticias/la-asamblea-extraordinaria-ratifica-por-mayoria-el-proyecto-laliga-impulso) |
